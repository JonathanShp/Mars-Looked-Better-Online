Walk this in Minecraft

The walk you plan here can be built as real Jezero ground in Minecraft Java Edition, 1 block = 1 metre of Mars, sideways and up. Block 0,0 is Perseverance's landing site.

  1. On the computer with Minecraft, open the game (the title screen is fine) and run Start MarsCuff Minecraft.bat (it needs the mcmcp mod and Python with numpy). The first run makes a world called MarsCuff Jezero holding a 3 km disc of ground around the landing site — about a minute.
  2. It opens this page again at http://localhost:8770. There the ⛏ Send to Minecraft button appears, and the ground that would be built is shaded yellow along your route.
  3. Plan a walk anywhere on the map and press that button. Minecraft closes, the ground along your route is written, and it opens again (30–60 s) with you in the habitat: glowing arrows for the route, beams for home, the stations and the Point of No Return, and the cuff as a written book in your hand. In the zipped world (below) the same markers are built into the ground as tall pillars, so they need no mod.

Why the world stops at the edge: only the ground near your walk is built — 500 m either side of the line, 800 m around the habitat and each station, about 100 MB a walk. The whole 900 km² map at 1 m per block would be some 14 GB. Plan a different walk and send it again to get different ground.

No mod on the other computer? A built world zips up as an ordinary save with a HOW-TO-USE.txt inside: make_world.py --route plan.json --save <save> --zip MarsCuff.zip. Details in minecraft/README.md.
