Download a Marswalk

Three ready-made Minecraft worlds built from NASA/USGS maps of Jezero Crater, where Perseverance landed in 2021. One block is one metre of Mars, sideways and up, so the hills are the real hills. No mod, no add-on and no Python needed — they are ordinary Minecraft Java Edition 26.3 saves. Unzip into your saves folder and open.

What you actually do in there

You get an air budget — 384 minutes, counted down above your hotbar, one EVA minute every 10 real seconds, so a full walk is about an hour of playing. That budget covers walking and working and the walk home, which is the whole problem: stop too long at the second station and you do not make it back. Follow the line of glowing blocks. A white pillar is home, a purple pillar is a science station, a red one is the Point of No Return. Stand at a station and your cuff prints the jobs for that rock — the photo, the sample, the abrasion, the chemistry — in chat.

The ground, the rock units and the rover's track are real data. The walking speed (Tobler's hiking function slowed to 0.6 for a suit), the 30 minutes of work per station and the time compression are a model and our own team assumptions, not a NASA timeline.

Contacts — 7.5 km

4:00 of walking and working · 6:24 of air · 3 stations · 15 MB

Every station sits on a mapped boundary between two rock units. Photographing a contact from both sides and sampling each side is how you learn which rock came first — which is how the order of events in Jezero gets settled. Start here.

Variety — 7.3 km

3:56 of walking and working · 6:24 of air · 3 stations · 15 MB

Each station is uncertain in a different way: one never driven, one mapped only at 20 m per pixel, one on a contact. Three different kinds of question in one walk.

Far Side — 10.9 km

5:09 of walking and working · 6:24 of air · 3 stations · 21 MB

The long one. Three different rock units, all of them more than 500 m from anywhere the rover has driven, so nobody has ever seen them from the ground. 5:09 against 6:24 of air leaves very little slack — watch the clock.

How to install

  1. Unzip the file. Inside is a world folder and a HOW-TO-USE.txt.
  2. Put the world folder in your saves folder: %APPDATA%\.minecraft\saves on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/saves on macOS, ~/.minecraft/saves on Linux.
  3. Open Minecraft Java Edition 26.3, Singleplayer, and the world is in the list. It warns about "Experimental Settings" because it carries its own data pack — that is the air clock and the Mars sky, so load it anyway.

Want your own route instead? Plan one on the map and read minecraft/README.md for how to build it.
