In the world

An air budget of 384 minutes counts down above your hotbar, one EVA minute every 10 real seconds, so a full walk is about an hour of play. Walking home costs air too.

Follow the glowing line. A white pillar is home, a purple one is a science station, a red one is the Point of No Return. Stand at a station and its real task list prints in chat.

The air timer and the in-world task lists are new, not yet tested in game.

The ground, the rock units and the rover track are data. The walking speed, the 30 minutes of work per station and the time compression are a model and our own team assumptions - not a NASA timeline.

Install

  1. Unzip.
  2. Put the world folder in your saves folder: %APPDATA%\.minecraft\saves on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/saves on macOS, ~/.minecraft/saves on Linux.
  3. Open Minecraft Java Edition 26.3, Singleplayer, pick the world.
  4. It warns about Experimental Settings - load it anyway. That is the world's own data pack: the Mars sky, the gravity and the air timer.
